Session: Sharing is caring: Open data for Open Source compliance

Open Source compliance depends on accurate data about millions of software components, yet organizations often duplicate efforts and build isolated datasets. ClearlyDefined offers a better approach: shared, community-verified open data on software component provenance, license, and vulnerabilities that improves accuracy, reduces duplication, and enables compliance automation at scale.
