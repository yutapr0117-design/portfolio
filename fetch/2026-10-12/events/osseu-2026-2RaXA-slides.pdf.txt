AI-Assisted Contributions Are Already Happening
AI is becoming part of contribution workflows.
• Code, tests, documentation, and issue responses may be created with AI assistance
• AI involvement can range from light assistance to substantial generation
• Communities need reviewable and accountable contributions
• Companies need explainable contributions before sending them upstream
The challenge is not simply whether AI was used.
The challenge is how to preserve trust.

Key Takeaways
• Project maintainers are seeing a workload increase driven by AI 
which reflects in a negative technology impact and maintainer 
burnout.
• AI benefits are discovered by Open Source community and 
contribution guidelines are updated to reflect the new way of 
working.
• We see potential to adapt existing frameworks to AI-assisted 
contributions, together with AI-specific controls.
• We believe governance should reflect AI autonomy, maintainer 
impact, and associated supply chain risk.
