Deep Dive Sessions Overview

For the first time ever, All Things Open will feature a dedicated Deep Dive session block.

Taking place on Monday, October 19, these extended two-hour sessions are designed for attendees who want to go beyond traditional conference talks and spend more time learning directly from experts.

Attendees can expect hands-on instruction, practical takeaways, and opportunities to engage with some of the most respected voices in technology and open source.

Deep Dive Session Pass

Date: Monday, October 19 • Doors Open: 7:30 am • Programming: 9:00 am – 4:30 pm

Your Deep Dive Session Pass gives you access to 2-3 deep dive session(s) during the day, with 15 sessions available to choose from.
