About Event

​Code & Compliance Community Day: Bridging the Regulatory Gap

​Join us in Brussels!

​Building Trust is the foundation of a resilient digital future. Join industry leaders and the open source community at Code & Compliance to bridge the critical knowledge gaps surrounding European legal compliance.

​Together, we will explore practical strategies for developing compliant products, navigating evolving EU regulatory frameworks (CRA, AI Act), and fostering cross-sector collaboration. Don't miss this opportunity to turn regulatory challenges into an engine for shared innovation, transparency, and lasting trust.

​Why join

​First CRA obligations start this September and more are coming in 12 months. Build compliance with confidence.

​Code & Compliance brings together manufacturers, open source leaders, legal experts, policymakers, and security professionals to tackle the real-world challenges of implementing European digital regulations.

​Learn how to put regulation into practice and reduce compliance risks

​Move beyond theory with practical guidance on implementing the Cyber Resilience Act (CRA), the AI Act, and other evolving European regulatory requirements, aligning engineering, legal, and security teams.

​Connect with the people shaping compliance

​Engage directly with regulators, industry leaders, open source maintainers, and compliance experts working to define best practices.

​Decode digital sovereignty and autonomy

​Understand how open source and open standards drive digital sovereignty and autonomy in practice.

​Influence the future

​Participate in collaborative sessions that contribute to guidance, working group initiatives, and future open source compliance efforts

​Who should join

  • ​Manufacturers & Product Teams: Companies across all industries that are actively building and deploying operational readiness plans for the CRA and AI Act.

  • ​Industry Associations: Trade organisations and sector bodies looking to learn how to better cooperate, align, and collaborate with the open source community.

  • ​ORC Working Group Members: Global open source and enterprise stakeholders collaborating on the front lines of regulatory standards.

  • ​Open Source Program Offices (OSPOs) & Tech Leaders: Internal teams tasked with governing open source adoption and securing the software supply chain.

  • ​Institutional & Policy Stakeholders: Policymakers and regulators looking to align European legal frameworks with practical development realities.

  • ​Software Security & Legal Compliance Professionals: Cross-functional experts translating complex legal requirements into practical, secure engineering workflows that build marketplace trust.
