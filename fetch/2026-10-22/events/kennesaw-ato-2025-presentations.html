Presentations

All Things Open 2025, hosted by the Kennesaw State University (KSU) Libraries and Texas Tech University (TTU) Libraries the week of April 7-10, is a series of events dedicated to promoting inclusive and intentional open access practices regardless of discipline. The theme of this year's conference is "Sustainable Growth."

Read the ATO2026 conference proceedings, hosted by the Journal of Open Educational Resources in Higher Education.
