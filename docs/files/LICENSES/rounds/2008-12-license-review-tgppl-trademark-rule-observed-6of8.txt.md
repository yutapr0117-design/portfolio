# LICENSES/rounds/2008-12-license-review-tgppl-trademark-rule-observed-6of8.txt

## What

**Transitive Grace Period Public Licence の審査**（`license-review` / `license-discuss`・
2008-12〜2009・全 134 通）の **6/8 部**。
**我々宛ではなく、ACD-1.0 についてでもない —— 観測である。**

## Why

**「OSD 適合は十分条件ではない」の理由が、当時の OSI 会長の言葉で述べられている唯一の場所である** ——
Russ Nelson 氏 *"There are **reasons beyond OSD conformance** why we might not want to approve the
license. Why? **Because we have a trademark to preserve.** If you can figure out some way to
**comply with the OSD in a way that does not achieve the desired open source effect**, which
endangers the meaning of OSI-Approved trademark, **then we MUST deny your license OSI approval
(trademark law gives us no choice)**."* + *"**Somebody has to decide what OSD conformance means.
It's us.**"*（#263）
