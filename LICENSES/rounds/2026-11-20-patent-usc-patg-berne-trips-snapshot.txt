===== 35 U.S.C. 271(a), (b), (c), as reported =====
1. "Except as otherwise provided in this title, whoever without authority makes, uses, offers to sell, or sells any patented"
2. "invention, within the United States or imports into the United States any patented invention"
3. "during the term of the patent therefor, infringes the patent."
4. "Whoever actively induces infringement of a patent shall be liable as an infringer."
5. "Whoever offers to sell or sells within the United States or imports into the United States a component of a patented machine"
6. ", manufacture, combination or composition, or a material or apparatus for use in practicing a patented process,"
7. " constituting a material part of the invention, knowing the same to be especially made"
8. " or especially adapted for use in an infringement of such patent,"
9. " and not a staple article or commodity of commerce suitable for substantial noninfringing use,"
10. " shall be liable as a contributory infringer."

===== 35 U.S.C. 261, second and fourth paragraphs, as reported =====
11. "Applications for patent, patents, or any interest therein, shall be assignable in law by an instrument in writing."
12. "The applicant, patentee, or his assigns or legal representatives may in like manner grant and convey an exclusive right"
13. "under his application for patent, or patents, to the whole or any specified part of the United States."
14. "An interest that constitutes an assignment, grant or conveyance shall be void as against any subsequent purchaser"
15. "or mortgagee for a valuable consideration, without notice, unless it is recorded in the Patent and Trademark Office"
16. "within three months from its date or prior to the date of such subsequent purchase or mortgage."

===== § 11 PatG, opening words and Nr. 1 and 2, as reported =====
17. "Die Wirkung des Patents erstreckt sich nicht auf"
18. "Handlungen, die im privaten Bereich zu nichtgewerblichen Zwecken vorgenommen werden;"
19. "Handlungen zu Versuchszwecken, die sich auf den Gegenstand der patentierten Erfindung beziehen;"

===== § 15 PatG, Abs. 1 Satz 1, Abs. 2 Satz 1, Abs. 3, as reported =====
20. "Das Recht auf das Patent, der Anspruch auf Erteilung des Patents und das Recht aus dem Patent gehen auf die Erben über."
21. "Die Rechte nach Absatz 1 können ganz oder teilweise Gegenstand von ausschließlichen oder nicht ausschließlichen Lizenzen"
22. "für den Geltungsbereich dieses Gesetzes oder einen Teil desselben sein."
23. "Ein Rechtsübergang oder die Erteilung einer Lizenz berührt nicht Lizenzen, die Dritten vorher erteilt worden sind."

===== Berne Convention, Article 5(2) and Article 6bis(1), as reported =====
24. "The enjoyment and the exercise of these rights shall not be subject to any formality;"
25. "such enjoyment and such exercise shall be independent of the existence of protection"
26. "in the country of origin of the work."
27. "Consequently, apart from the provisions of this Convention, the extent of protection,"
28. "as well as the means of redress afforded to the author to protect his rights,"
29. "shall be governed exclusively by the laws of the country where protection is claimed."
30. "Independently of the author's economic rights, and even after the transfer of the said rights,"
31. "the author shall have the right to claim authorship of the work and to object to any distortion,"
32. "mutilation or other modification of, or other derogatory action in relation to, the said work,"
33. "which would be prejudicial to his honor or reputation."

===== TRIPS Agreement, Article 28 and Article 30, as reported (footnote markers omitted by the tool) =====
34. "where the subject matter of a patent is a product, to prevent third parties not having the owner's consent"
35. "from the acts of: making, using, offering for sale, selling, or importing for these purposes that product;"
36. "where the subject matter of a patent is a process, to prevent third parties not having the owner's consent"
37. "from the act of using the process, and from the acts of: using, offering for sale, selling, or importing"
38. "for these purposes at least the product obtained directly by that process."
39. "Patent owners shall also have the right to assign, or transfer by succession, the patent"
40. "and to conclude licensing contracts."
41. "Members may provide limited exceptions to the exclusive rights conferred by a patent, provided that such exceptions"
42. "do not unreasonably conflict with a normal exploitation of the patent and do not unreasonably prejudice the"
43. "legitimate interests of the patent owner, taking account of the legitimate interests of third parties."
