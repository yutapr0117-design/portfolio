Initiative on the MIT No Attribution License. Pamela Chestek

Chair, License Committee Open Source Initiative *****

License: MIT No Attribution License (Exhibit A)

SPDX Identifier: MIT-0

Submitted: 15 May 2020, 
http://lists.opensource.org/pipermail/license-review_lists.opensource.org/2020-May/004856.html

Decision date: due no later than the first Board meeting after 15 July 2020


License Review Committee Recommendation:


Resolved that it is the opinion of the OSI that the MIT No Attribution 
License be approved as an Open Source Initiative Approved license in the 
Other/Miscellaneous category of licenses.


Rationale Document


The license is the same language as the MIT License except that the 
attribution requirement of the MIT License in its second paragraph is 
removed. The license was discussed on both the License-Discuss list and 
the License-Review list and no one raised a substantive objection to its 
approval. There was some discussion whether the license was actually in 
use, but this is not a basis for rejection that the OSI has applied in 
the past (and the OSI has historically approved newly drafted licenses 
not yet in use).


It is classified in the ?Other/Miscellaneous? category because the 
0-clause BSD License is in the same category. Both licenses have the 
same modification to their origin license, which is to remove the 
requirement that the copyright notice and permission notice be included 
in copies of the software.
