> This does open another process question: to date the process to 
> develop OSAID has happened independently of L-R. Presumably this

The OSI does not plan to create documents that implement the OSAID but 
expects the review process will function as the software license 
approval currently does, through submission.
>
>
>> Finally, I think we'll want to list approved documents which are not
>> software licenses separately on the website from the software licenses.
>
> I'd wondered about this. "Under OSD-conformant terms" looks like a 
> mistake. 
It's a term we formerly used, but have now adopted "OSI-approved terms" 
instead for non-software components. The reason to use the word "terms" 
instead of "license" is because the documents may assure the necessary 
rights through the use of legal mechanisms other than a license.
