> *License Review Committee Recommendation: *
>
> Resolved that it is the opinion of the OSI that the W3C Software and 
> Document license - 2023 version is not a new license. The text on the 
> OSI website will be modified to reflect that the license has 
> alternative text for the names of the copyright owners."
>
> Rationale Document
>
> The license-review list criticized this license here and here for its 
> reference to external documents (?Permission to copy ? provided that 
> you include the following on ALL copies of the work or portions 
> thereof, including modifications: Any pre-existing intellectual 
> property disclaimers, notices, or terms and conditions?) that might 
> add unacceptable limitations, including the withholding of patent 
> licenses. However, this license was approved in 2017, despite the 
> objections also being voiced then.
>
> SPDX, which has established standards for determining when changes to 
> a license create a new version, said ?In 2023, W3C made minor 
> modifications to this license as of Jan 1, 2023?. However, those 
> changes did not give rise to a new license under the SPDX Matching 
> Guidelines, thus the same id can be used.?
>
> In order to maintain consistency with OSI?s past decisions and with 
> SPDX (thus also avoiding the challenge of having an OSI-approved 
> license that would not have a unique SPDX identifier), the OSI will 
> also consider this license the same license as the 2015 version and an 
> OSI-approved license.
