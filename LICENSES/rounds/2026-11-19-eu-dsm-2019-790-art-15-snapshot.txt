===== Directive (EU) 2019/790 Article 15 (Protection of press publications concerning online uses) =====
15(1) "Member States shall provide publishers of press publications established in a Member State with the rights provided"
      [...] "for the online use of their press publications by information society service providers."
15(2) "The rights provided for in paragraph 1 shall leave intact and shall in no way affect any rights provided"
      [...] "for in Union law to authors and other rightholders, in respect of the works and other subject matter incorporated"
      [...] "in a press publication."
