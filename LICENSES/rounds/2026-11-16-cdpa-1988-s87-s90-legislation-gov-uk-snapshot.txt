===== section 87 (Consent and waiver of rights), (1) to (4) =====
1. "(1)It is not an infringement of any of the rights conferred by this Chapter to do any act to which the person entitled"
2. "to the right has consented."
3. "(2)Any of those rights may be waived by instrument in writing signed by the person giving up the right."
4. "(3)A waiver—"
5. "(a)may relate to a specific work, to works of a specified description or to works generally, and may relate to existing"
6. "or future works, and"
7. "(b)may be conditional or unconditional and may be expressed to be subject to revocation;"
8. "and if made in favour of the owner or prospective owner of the copyright in the work or works to which it relates,"
9. "it shall be presumed to extend to his licensees and successors in title unless a contrary intention is expressed."
10. "(4)Nothing in this Chapter shall be construed as excluding the operation of the general law of contract or estoppel"
11. "in relation to an informal waiver or other transaction in relation to any of the rights mentioned in subsection (1)."

===== section 90 (Assignment and licences), (1), (3) and (4) =====
12. "Copyright is transmissible by assignment, by testamentary disposition or by operation of law, as personal"
13. "or moveable property."
14. "An assignment of copyright is not effective unless it is in writing signed by or on behalf of the assignor."
15. "A licence granted by a copyright owner is binding on every successor in title to his interest in the copyright,"
16. "except a purchaser in good faith for valuable consideration and without notice (actual or constructive) of the licence"
17. "or a person deriving title from such a purchaser; and references in this Part to doing anything with, or without,"
18. "the licence of the copyright owner shall be construed accordingly."
