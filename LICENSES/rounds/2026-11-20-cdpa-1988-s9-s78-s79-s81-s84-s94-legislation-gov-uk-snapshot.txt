WHY THIS FILE EXISTS. Section 12 of the 1.4 draft waives moral rights to the extent the law permits (12.1) and covenants
and consents for every jurisdiction (12.2); Section 11.3 leaves false attribution outside the Dedication; trial T122 asks
whether the waiver in 12.1 should reach the right against false attribution. These sections say which UK moral rights
apply to computer programs and computer-generated works, and how they are asserted.

WHAT THIS FILE IS NOT. It is not legal advice and not a statement about ACD.

===== section 9(1) and (3) =====
1. "In this Part "author", in relation to a work, means the person who creates it."
2. "In the case of a literary, dramatic, musical or artistic work which is computer-generated,"
3. "the author shall be taken to be the person by whom the arrangements necessary for the creation of the work are undertaken."

===== section 78(1) and (2) (requirement that right be asserted) =====
4. "(1)A person does not infringe the right conferred by section 77 (right to be identified as author or director)"
5. "by doing any of the acts mentioned in that section unless the right has been asserted"
6. "in accordance with the following provisions so as to bind him in relation to that act."
7. "(2)The right may be asserted generally, or in relation to any specified act or description of acts—"
8. "(a)on an assignment of copyright in the work, by including in the instrument effecting the assignment"
9. "a statement that the author or director asserts in relation to that work his right to be identified, or"
10. "(b)by instrument in writing signed by the author or director."

===== section 79(1) and (2) (exceptions to right to be identified) =====
11. "The right conferred by section 77 (right to be identified as author or director) is subject to the following exceptions."
12. "The right does not apply in relation to the following descriptions of work—"
13. "(a) a computer program;"
14. "(b) the design of a typeface;"
15. "(c) any computer-generated work."

===== section 81(1) and (2) (exceptions to right to object to derogatory treatment) =====
16. "The right conferred by section 80 (right to object to derogatory treatment of work) is subject to the following exceptions."
17. "The right does not apply to a computer program or to any computer-generated work."

===== section 84(1), (2), (3), (5) and (6) (false attribution of work) =====
18. "(1)A person has the right in the circumstances mentioned in this section—"
19. "(a)not to have a literary, dramatic, musical or artistic work falsely attributed to him as author, and"
20. "(b)not to have a film falsely attributed to him as director;"
21. "and in this section an "attribution", in relation to such a work, means a statement (express or implied)"
22. "as to who is the author or director."
23. "(2)The right is infringed by a person who—"
24. "(a)issues to the public copies of a work of any of those descriptions in or on which there is a false attribution, or"
25. "(b)exhibits in public an artistic work, or a copy of an artistic work, in or on which there is a false attribution."
26. "(3)The right is also infringed by a person who—"
27. "(a)in the case of a literary, dramatic or musical work, performs the work in public or communicates it to the public"
28. "as being the work of a person, or"
29. "(b)in the case of a film, shows it in public or communicates it to the public as being directed by a person,"
30. "knowing or having reason to believe that the attribution is false."
31. "(5)The right is also infringed by a person who in the course of a business—"
32. "(a)possesses or deals with a copy of a work of any of the descriptions mentioned in subsection (1)"
33. "in or on which there is a false attribution, or"
34. "(b)in the case of an artistic work, possesses or deals with the work itself when there is a false attribution in or on it,"
35. "knowing or having reason to believe that there is such an attribution and that it is false."
(Subsection (6), on altered artistic works dealt with as unaltered, was also fetched and is not relevant here.)

===== section 94 (moral rights not assignable) =====
36. "The rights conferred by Chapter IV (moral rights) are not assignable."
