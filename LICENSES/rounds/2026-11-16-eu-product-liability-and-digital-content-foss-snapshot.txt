WHY THIS FILE EXISTS. Sections 13 (no warranty) and 14 (limitation of liability) of the 1.4 draft rely on
being able to disclaim and limit. `review-readings-2.md` §1.258 recorded that a limitation "to the extent
permitted by applicable law" may be disregarded altogether in some countries. In the EU two instruments
decide, before any disclaimer is read, whether a free supplier is exposed at all: strict product
liability, which no contract can exclude, and consumer law on digital content. Both carry an express
exclusion for free and open-source software. The dossier did not hold either text.
