the two questions this thread asks -- can copyright be abandoned, and what survives a grant
that says it is irrevocable -- are the two questions the instrument stands on. The dossier had
analysed both from the statutes (jurisdictions.md 5a and 5b). It had not read the list's own
treatment, which names the same US provision and adds a distributor's practical position on
what a clear dedication does even where abandonment is uncertain.
