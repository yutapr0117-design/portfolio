===== 17 U.S.C. §1201 (Circumvention of copyright protection systems) =====
(a)(1)(A) "No person shall circumvent a technological measure that effectively controls access to a work protected under this title."
(a)(3)(A) "to “circumvent a technological measure” means to descramble a scrambled work, to decrypt an encrypted work,"
          "or otherwise to avoid, bypass, remove, deactivate, or impair a technological measure,"
          "without the authority of the copyright owner; and"
(a)(3)(B) "a technological measure “effectively controls access to a work” if the measure, in the ordinary course of its operation,"
          "requires the application of information, or a process or a treatment, with the authority of the copyright owner,"
          "to gain access to the work."
