       Submission name: Mulan OWLs v1 are a new member of Mulan         license family, in which Mulan PSL v2 has been approved by the         OSI in 2020. Mulan OWLs v1 are a suite of four licenses:
       - Mulan Open Works License Attribution, Version 1 (MulanOWL         BY v1): http://license.coscl.org.cn/MulanOWLBYv1
       - Mulan Open Works License Attribution-ShareAlike, Version 1         (MulanOWL BY-SA v1): http://license.coscl.org.cn/MulanOWLBYSAv1
       - Mulan Open Works License Attribution-PatentLicensed,         Version 1 (MulanOWL BY-PL v1): http://license.coscl.org.cn/MulanOWLBYPLv1
       - Mulan Open Works License         Attribution-PatentLicensed-ShareAlike, Version 1 (MulanOWL         BY-PL-SA v1): http://license.coscl.org.cn/MulanOWLBYPLSAv1
       
       
       
       
       Rationale:
       1) Nowadays, the idea and production mode of open source have         been extended to other types of works. We present two real         examples below:
       
       
       - Example 1: users have been increasingly utilizing the         GitHub infrastructure to collaboratively create open tutorials         and learning materials, such as technical interview tutorials         (e.g., https://github.com/CyC2018/CS-Notes)         and programming materials (https://github.com/lydiahallie/javascript-questions)         [see our research paper: Yao Lu et al., Studying in the         ?Bazaar?: an Exploratory Study of Crowdsourced Learning in         GitHub]. Many of these non-software projects have become as         popular as well-known software projects, especially that the         most-stars Java project in GitHub, CyC2018/CS-Notes, is a         non-software project.
       
       
       - Example 2: Chinese designers (mainly for UI design) have         recently proposed the initiative of Open Design in 2022 (https://view.inews.qq.com/k/20220509A04SIY00?web_channel=wap&amp;openApp=false&amp;pgv_ref=baidutw).         The core values of Open Design are: open copyright, active         ecosystems, and collaboratively creating. These values stem from         the idea of open source and have made a significant impact in         the field of design: the popular Chinese design platform,         js.design (https://js.design/community?category=explore),         has accumulated a considerable number of open design works, and         designers can easily create adapted works based on existing open         design works on the site.
       
       
       Inheriting the idea of open source software, these works have         the following characteristics: a) open use, b) free         distribution, and c) allowing modification, which we call open         works. Like open source software license, open works license is         a format contract with legal effect that regulates and restricts         the copying, use, modification and distribution of open works.         With the extension of open source in practice, the concept of         ?source? can be extended and explained as ?source file?, so as         to include more types of works. We expect that the non-software         open licenses which conform with the spirit of open source and         can promote the development of open source can be included in         the OSI-approved licenses. We note that the OSI has already         approved licenses which are not purely for software, e.g., open         font (IPA and OFL-1.1) and open hardware (CERN-OHLs-2.0), which         we believe is in line with this trend.
       
       
       2) We observe that right holders of open works present         requirement for granting patent license, which can be reflected         from the following two well-known cases:
       
       
       - Case 1: In 2014, US electric car maker Tesla Motors Inc         opened its patents for all to use through a ?Patent Pledge? (https://www.tesla.cn/legal/additional-resources).         The patents are mainly regarding to the manufacture of electric         cars. People can download and use the related patent files.         These files are textual works rather than software code.
       
       
       - Case 2: In 2020, Medtronic opened design specifications for         the Puritan BennettTM 560 (PB560) ventilator to allow         innovators, inventors, start-ups, and academic institutions to         manufacture ventilators (https://www.medtronic.com/us-en/e/open-files.html).         The design specifications are mainly design documents including         textual files and drawing files. These materials are licensed         under a special permissive license that is drafted by Medtronic         (https://www.medtronic.com/content/dam/medtronic-com/global/Corporate/covid19/documents/permissive-license-open-ventilator.pdf),         which contains the grant of patent license. This case is related         with the ?Open Covid Pledge? and the ?Open COVID License ?         Patent and Copyright (OCL-PC)? (https://opencovidpledge.org/v1-1-ocl-pc/),         which also refers to granting patent license.
       
       
       Additionally, the posted UI designs in the Example 2 in         section 1) may also refer to granting design patent license.
       
       
       Therefore, open works license should keep with users? new         requirements and include terms of granting license of patent         covered by the copyrighted portion of the open works.
       
       
       &nbsp;
       Distinguish:
       As the name suggests, Mulan OWLs can be applied to general         works under copyright law, so there is no existing OSI-approved         license to directly compare and contrast it with. The set of         licenses, of course, can be used for software (but not         suggested) and thus are probably most closely comparable to         Apache License 2.0, BSD 3-clause License and Mulan PSL v2.         However, these licenses are not suitable for other types of         works.
       
       
       Overall, the main charateristics of Mulan OWLs v1 are         summarized below:
       - The licenses are applicable for open works (as explained in         section 1));
       - The licenses include two licenses that grant patent license         (MulanOWL BY-PL v1 and MulanOWL BY-PL-SA v1);
       - The licenses provide conventions of attribution for         multiversion works, which facilitates the use and retrieval of         works that are frequently modified, such as the crowdsourced         works mentioned in Example 1;
       - As with Mulan PSL v2, Mulan OWLs are written in both         Chinese and English, which have the same legal effect. In case         of divergence between the Chinese and English versions, the         Chinese version shall prevail.
       
       
       
       
       Legal review:
       As with Mulan PSL, Mulan OWLs v1 were drafted by experts and         lawyers from colleges, research institutions, enterprises and         open source foundations. The lawyers are open source legal         experts specialized in the field of intellectual property and         open source software. Furthermore, the licenses have also been         reviewed by two third-party law firms on and an open source         legal expert in a company to ensure the license quality in         legality and written.
