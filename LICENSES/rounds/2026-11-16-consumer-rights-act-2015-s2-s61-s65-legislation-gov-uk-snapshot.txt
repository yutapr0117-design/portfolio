WHY THIS FILE EXISTS. UCTA 1977 section 2(4) sends consumer contracts and consumer notices to this Act
(rounds/2026-11-16-ucta-1977-s1-s2-legislation-gov-uk-snapshot.txt). Whether Part 2 reaches a Dedicator turns on
"trader" and "consumer notice"; section 65 is the counterpart of UCTA section 2(1) for Section 14.1 of the 1.4 draft.

WHAT THIS FILE IS NOT. It is not legal advice and not a statement about ACD.

===== section 2(2) and (3) =====
1. "(2)"Trader" means a person acting for purposes relating to that person's trade, business, craft or profession,"
2. "whether acting personally or through another person acting in the trader's name or on the trader's behalf."
3. "(3)"Consumer" means an individual acting for purposes that are wholly or mainly outside that individual's trade,"
4. "business, craft or profession."

===== section 61(1) to (4) and (7) =====
5. "This Part applies to a contract between a trader and a consumer."
6. "This does not include a contract of employment or apprenticeship."
7. "A contract to which this Part applies is referred to in this Part as a "consumer contract"."
8. "This Part applies to a notice to the extent that it—"
9. "(a)relates to rights or obligations as between a trader and a consumer, or"
10. "(b)purports to exclude or restrict a trader's liability to a consumer."
11. "A notice to which this Part applies is referred to in this Part as a "consumer notice"."

===== section 65(1) and (2) =====
12. "A trader cannot by a term of a consumer contract or by a consumer notice exclude or restrict liability for death or"
13. "personal injury resulting from negligence."
14. "Where a term of a consumer contract, or a consumer notice, purports to exclude or restrict a trader's liability"
15. "for negligence, a person is not to be taken to have voluntarily accepted any risk merely because the person"
16. "agreed to or knew about the term or notice."
