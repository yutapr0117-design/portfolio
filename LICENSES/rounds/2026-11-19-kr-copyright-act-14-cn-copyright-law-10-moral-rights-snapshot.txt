===== Copyright Act of the Republic of Korea Article 14 (Inalienability of Author's Moral Right) =====
(1) "(1) Author’s moral rights shall belong exclusively to the author."
(2) "(2) Even after the death of the author, no person who use his/her work shall commit an act"
    "which would be prejudicial to author’s moral rights if he/she were alive:"
    "Provided, That if such act is deemed to have not defamed the honor of the author"
    "in the light of the nature and extent of the act, and in view of the prevailing social norms, the same shall not apply."
（注）The page (Korea Copyright Commission's English translation) shows no consolidated version date for Article 14.

===== Copyright Law of the People's Republic of China (2020) Article 10 (moral rights; licensing and transfer of items (5) to (17)) =====
(1) "the right of publication, that is, the right to decide whether to make a work available to the public;"
(2) "the right of authorship, that is, the right to claim authorship"
    "to have the author's name mentioned in connection with the work;"
(3) "the right of alteration, that is, the right to alter or authorize others to alter one's work;"
(4) "the right of integrity, that is, the right to protect one's work against distortion and mutilation;"
(licensing) "Copyright owners may authorize others to exercise the rights provided in Subparagraphs (5) to (17)"
    "and receive remuneration in accordance with the agreements or the relevant provisions of this Law."
(transfer) "Copyright owners may transfer, wholly or in part, the rights provided in Subparagraphs (5) to (17)"
    "of the first paragraph of this Article and receive remuneration in accordance with the agreements or the relevant provisions of this Law."
（注）The NPC page labels the text "Translation for Reference Only". Items (1) to (4) are not among the rights the last two paragraphs allow to be licensed or transferred.
