===== section 33 (duration of copyright in original works) =====

1. "70 years after the calendar year in which the author of the work died."

===== section 40(1) and (2) (fair dealing for purpose of research or study) =====
2. "A fair dealing with a literary, dramatic, musical or artistic work, or with an adaptation of a literary,"
3. "dramatic or musical work, for the purpose of research or study does not constitute an infringement"
4. "of the copyright in the work."
5. "the purpose and character of the dealing;"
6. "the nature of the work or adaptation;"
7. "the possibility of obtaining the work or adaptation within a reasonable time at an ordinary commercial price;"
8. "the effect of the dealing upon the potential market for, or value of, the work or adaptation; and"
9. "in a case where part only of the work or adaptation is reproduced—the amount and substantiality of the part copied"
10. "taken in relation to the whole work or adaptation."

===== section 195AM(2) (duration of author's moral rights) =====
11. "An author’s right of integrity of authorship in respect of a work other than a cinematograph film"
12. "continues in force until copyright ceases to subsist in the work."

===== section 195AN(1) and (3) (exercise of author's moral rights) =====
13. "If the author of a work dies, the author’s moral rights (other than the right of integrity of authorship"
14. "in respect of a cinematograph film) in respect of the work may be exercised and enforced by his or her"
15. "legal personal representative."
16. "Subject to this section, a moral right in respect of a work is not transmissible by assignment, by will,"
17. "or by devolution by operation of law."

===== section 195AWA(2) to (5) (author's consent to act or omission) =====
18. "It is not an infringement of a moral right of an author in respect of a work to do, or omit to do,"
19. "something if the act or omission is within the scope of a written consent genuinely given by the author"
20. "or a person representing the author."
21. "Subject to subsection (4), a consent does not have any effect unless it is given:"
22. "(a) in relation to specified acts or omissions, or specified classes or types of acts or omissions,"
23. "whether occurring before or after the consent is given; and"
24. "(b) in relation to either of the following:"
25. "(i) a specified work or specified works existing when the consent is given; or"
26. "(ii) a specified work, or works of a particular description, the making of which has not begun"
27. "or that is or are in the course of being made."
28. "A consent may be given by an employee for the benefit of his or her employer in relation to all or any"
29. "acts or omissions (whether occurring before or after the consent is given) and in relation to all works"
30. "made or to be made by the employee in the course of his or her employment."
31. "A consent given for the benefit of the owner or prospective owner of copyright in the work or works"
32. "to which it relates is presumed, unless the contrary intention appears in the consent instrument,"
33. "to extend to his or her licensees and successors in title, and to any persons who are authorised by"
34. "the owner or prospective owner, or by such a licensee or successor in title, to do acts comprised in"
35. "the copyright."

===== section 195AX (acts or omissions outside Australia) =====
36. "It is not an infringement of an author’s moral right in respect of a work"
37. "to do, or omit to do, something outside Australia."

===== section 196(1), (3) and (4) (assignments and licences in respect of copyright) =====
38. "Copyright is personal property and, subject to this section, is transmissible by assignment,"
39. "by will and by devolution by operation of law."
40. "An assignment of copyright (whether total or partial) does not have effect unless it is in writing"
41. "signed by or on behalf of the assignor."
42. "A licence granted in respect of a copyright by the owner of the copyright binds every successor"
43. "in title to the interest in the copyright of the grantor of the licence to the same extent as the"
44. "licence was binding on the grantor."

===== section 197(1) (prospective ownership of copyright) =====
45. "Where, by an agreement made in relation to a future copyright and signed by or on behalf of the"
46. "person who would, apart from this section, be the owner of the copyright on its coming into existence,"
47. "the copyright, on its coming into existence, vests in the assignee or his or her successor in title"
48. "by force of this subsection."
