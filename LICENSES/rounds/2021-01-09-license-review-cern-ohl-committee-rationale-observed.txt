License Review Committee Recommendation:

/Resolved that it is the opinion of the OSI that the CERN Open Hardware 
License Version 2 - Strongly Reciprocal, CERN Open Hardware License 
Version 2 - Weakly Reciprocal,and CERN Open Hardware License Version 2 - 
Permissive, conform to the OSD and assure software freedom and the 
licenses therefore be approved as an Open Source Initiative Certified 
license in the Special Purpose category of licenses./

_Rationale Document_

Notes: There was discussion about whether the licenses, primarily 
intended for hardware, were appropriate for review by the OSI. The 
intention of the license drafters was to create a single license that 
could be used for designs that includes both hardware and software. 
Although software is not the primary use case for the license, and the 
licenses include some unusual terminology that make the licenses more 
difficult to interpret, they are written such that they can be used for 
software, so they are appropriate for the OSI?s review.

One reviewer (the Chair of the License Committee) interpreted the Strong 
Reciprocal version of the license as requiring the disclosure of 
?Covered Source? that might be an independent component. The license 
drafters? explanation for why the license did not impose that duty, 
http://lists.opensource.org/pipermail/license-review_lists.opensource.org/2020-November/004958.html, 
satisfied the reviewer that her interpretation was incorrect.

Two members of the license-review list stated affirmatively that the 
licenses comply with the OSD and should be approved: 
http://lists.opensource.org/pipermail/license-review_lists.opensource.org/2020-July/004914.html, 
http://lists.opensource.org/pipermail/license-review_lists.opensource.org/2020-August/004916.html. 
There were no responses to a specific request by the Chair of the 
License Committee for objections to approval. 
http://lists.opensource.org/pipermail/license-review_lists.opensource.org/2020-December/004992.html 


The License Committee therefore recommends that the licenses be approved.
