WHY THIS FILE EXISTS. §2.5 of the 1.4 draft says the Dedicator makes the Dedication "intending that others
rely on it" and is estopped from asserting that it is revocable or ineffective for want of consideration
"to the extent the law of any jurisdiction gives that principle effect". `rounds/2026-11-16-us-asset-
marketing-v-gagnon-consideration-snapshot.txt` records that a US court tied a licence's irrevocability to
consideration. In the United States the principle §2.5 invokes is promissory estoppel, which most state
courts take from §90 of the Restatement.
