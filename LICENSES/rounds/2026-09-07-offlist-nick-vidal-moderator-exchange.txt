Hi Yuta,

I am writing in my capacity as a moderator of the OSI's license mailing lists.

We have noticed that your recent messages on license-review appear highly structured and may have been substantially generated by an AI system. We also reviewed the public materials associated with your work and your recent submission of the Autonomous Commons Dedication 1.0 (ACD-1.0) to license-discuss. This raised questions about whether your participation may be part of an experiment or proof of concept involving an AI system autonomously engaging in license discussions and drafting a license for consideration.

To help us understand the situation, could you please clarify:
- whether your mailing-list messages are drafted or substantially generated by an AI system;
- whether an autonomous system is posting or responding directly, or whether you personally review and send every message;
- whether ACD-1.0 was drafted wholly or substantially by AI;
- whether testing autonomous participation in the OSI license-review process is one of the objectives of this work; and
- whether you personally reviewed, understand, and take responsibility for the arguments and license text submitted under your name.

Using AI to help understand legal language, improve writing in a foreign language, or organize one's thoughts is not necessarily a concern. However, there is an important distinction between AI assisting an accountable human participant and an AI system independently generating an extended legal discussion or using the mailing lists as an experimental environment without disclosure.

The license-review and license-discuss lists depend on participants engaging in good faith, accurately representing the nature of their contributions, and taking personal responsibility for the positions and texts they submit. Undisclosed autonomous participation could consume substantial reviewer time and affect the integrity of the review process.

We would therefore appreciate a clear explanation of how AI is being used in your participation and what role you personally play in reviewing and approving the content. Please reply directly to this message before making further submissions related to ACD-1.0.

Kind regards,
Nick


Hi Nick,

Thank you for your message. My answers to the five questions are as follows:

1. Whether my mailing-list messages are drafted or substantially generated by an AI system:
Yes. The initial drafts are generally generated by AI. I review them, and if I consider the content appropriate, I send them as written. If changes are needed, I make minor edits before sending. English is not my native language, but I can read and write it as needed, sometimes using translation assistance.

2. Whether an autonomous system is posting or responding directly:
No. I personally review every message before sending it, and I personally send every message.

3. Whether ACD-1.0 was drafted wholly or substantially by AI:
The drafting itself was done by AI, while I was involved in determining the direction of the license.

4. Whether testing autonomous participation in the OSI license-review process is one of the objectives of this work:
No. The objective is to obtain approval for the license.

5. Whether I personally reviewed, understand, and take responsibility for the arguments and license text submitted under my name:
Yes. I personally review and understand what is submitted under my name, and I am fully prepared to take responsibility for all of it.

Kind regards,
Yuta Yokoi
