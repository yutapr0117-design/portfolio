WHY THIS FILE EXISTS. CC0 has the same three-part structure as §3 to §5 of the 1.4 draft (surrender,
licence, non-assertion). How the steward of that structure itself explains irrevocability, the licence
that takes effect where the waiver does not, moral rights, and patents is the public interpretation
`ACD-1.4-THIRD-CLARITY-TRIAL.md` §18 asks us to set beside each clause.
