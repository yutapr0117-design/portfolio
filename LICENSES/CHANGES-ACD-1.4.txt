ACD-1.4 -- CHANGES FROM ACD-1.3 (DRAFT). Not part of the Dedication.

  This list was kept at the top of LICENSES/ACD-1.4-DRAFT.txt until 2026-11-20,
  when it moved here so that the draft holds the text and its status only. The
  reasons for each change are in LICENSES/ACD-1.4-CHANGELIST.md (in Japanese).

  Changes in this draft, relative to ACD-1.3:

    * The Preamble now says that Section 3 surrenders "the Covered Rights",
      where it said "the rights" (errata E55), and names the three places as
      those "where that assumption fails", the assumption its first paragraph
      states, instead of judging the existing tools. (-3 words.)
    * Section 1.2 now says that Moral Rights include any right of a personal
      character conferred on an author by law "now or in future", the words
      Sections 1.5 and 8.1 use (errata E50). (+4 words.)
    * Section 1.3 no longer says "in whole or in part" (errata E61). (-5
      words.)
    * Section 1.5 now says "Covered Rights exclude", where it said "Covered
      Rights excludes" (errata E51). (0 words.)
    * Section 1.9 now reaches material submitted to the Dedicator or to any
      repository or forum "in either case offering the Work with a notice of
      this Dedication to the public or to those invited to contribute", where
      it reached any submission to the Dedicator or to a repository or forum
      the Dedicator maintains (errata E65, E73). (+18 words.)
    * Section 2.2 now bars a further term, condition, or restriction "on what
      may be done with" any copy of the Work that has been made available,
      where it barred one "in respect of" such a copy. (+3 words.)
    * Section 2.3 now says that to hold a permission You need not know of this
      Dedication. (+7 words.)
    * Section 2.6 now undertakes to identify any material that Section 1.9
      excludes from being a Contribution, binds only "a Dedicator who includes
      that material with the Work, or causes it to be included", and
      identifies the material "as a notice may identify material under
      Section 16.2". (+15 words.)
    * Section 2.7 now limits the rights reached to those the Dedicator holds
      "now or in future", the words Sections 8.1 and 8.4 use (errata E60), and
      no longer points to Section 13.2. (-4 words.)
    * Sections 2.8 and 12.4 no longer state why they bind successors
      expressly, and Section 2.8 now binds successors and transferees "in
      respect of the Work" and "to that extent" (errata E46, E49, E79). (-77
      words.)
    * Section 2.9 now says that nothing in this Dedication is, "under its
      terms", conditioned on, suspended by, or revived by an insolvency or an
      officer's act. (+3 words.)
    * Section 4.2 now names study, inspection, analysis, and reverse
      engineering among the acts the licence includes. (+5 words.)
    * Section 4.3 now names the size and revenue of the user, the scale of use,
      and the number of users served among the things the licence is not
      conditioned on. (+13 words.)
    * Section 4.6 now begins "Section 4.5 does not cut", where it began
      "Section 4.5 concerns the terms You may add. It does not cut". (-7
      words.)
    * Section 5.1 now gives its two covenants as two sentences, and the second
      reaches a claim "for" a use of the Work that this Dedication permits,
      where it reached one "arising from or relating to" such a use, and
      excepts patent infringement, trademark rights and rights in a name, a
      false statement or representation, and a payment You agreed to make;
      Section 5.2(d) excepts the false statement too. (+40 words.)
    * Section 5.2(a) no longer covenants that the Dedicator will not apply a
      technological measure, only that it will not assert rights in respect of
      one or invoke the law of circumvention against You, as GPL-3.0 Section 3
      does; a rate limit on the Dedicator's own server, a paid download, or a
      store's protection now falls outside it (errata E82). (-2 words.)
    * Section 5.2 now names an acceptable-use policy in paragraph (b);
      paragraph (d) now also covenants not to authorise or assist anyone else
      to assert the Dedicator's rights of privacy, publicity, or personality,
      with its exception for a false statement placed before the right
      asserted; and the closing sentence places the Dedicator's own material
      outside Section 5 (errata E67, E68, E81). Section 6.4 now says that You
      owe nothing "under this Dedication" (errata E70). (+82 words.)
    * Section 5.1 now reaches a claim "that treats as wrongful, or as without
      legal basis," a use of the Work that this Dedication permits, where it
      reached a claim "for" such a use, and Section 5.2 now says that a copy
      of the Work, or of part of it, contained in the Dedicator's other
      material "remains the Work, or that part of it". (+30 words.)
    * Section 5.3 now also names the covenant in Section 8.6 (errata E83). (+1
      word.)
    * Section 5.3 no longer lists the persons its covenants bind; it says
      that they "bind the persons Section 2.8 names, to the fullest extent
      the law permits", the limit Section 2.8 states. (-8 words.)
    * Section 2.8 now names a transferee of "a patent claim that Section 8
      reaches", and Section 12.4 binds "the persons Section 2.8 names" "to the
      fullest extent the law permits". (-6 words.)
    * Section 6.5 now says that silence on this subject "leaves it to be
      inferred whether this is permitted". (+4 words.)
    * Section 10.4 now says that no permission terminates "under it", where it
      said that none terminates for any reason. (+2 words.)
    * Section 11.3 no longer gives a reason for its limit, and Section 9.4
      says that Section 11.3 "governs a false claim of authorship", where it
      said that Section 11.3 explains why. (-18 words.)
    * Section 6.1 now permits Computational Use as to the patent claims
      Section 8 reaches, where it named the patent claims of the Dedicator and
      so went beyond Section 8. (+4 words.)
    * Section 6.4 now speaks of a model or output "resulting from"
      Computational Use of the Work, the words Section 8.4 uses, where it said
      "derived from", and no longer points to Section 9, which speaks of parts
      of the Work and not of models or outputs (errata E47). (-2 words.)
    * Sections 8.1 and 8.4 now license the acts themselves, ending "otherwise
      practise or transfer", and Section 8.1 reaches "the Work or anything
      derived from it" (errata E40, E41, E64). Section 8.4 now leaves a
      feature added to the Work to Section 8.1, reaches a claim only where the
      infringing feature results from the content of the Work, and no longer
      states its reasons (errata E56, E66, E78). (-103 words.)
    * Section 8.3 now says once that Sections 8.1 and 8.4 reach a patent claim
      only as to the acts they describe, where it spoke of any patent right of
      the Dedicator in respect of the Work, and no longer gives a reason
      naming software or a rule against readings that produce that result
      (errata E85). (-37 words.)
    * Section 8.5 now limits the claims reached to those the Dedicator owns or
      controls "now or in future", and says that a claim is controlled "if, and
      only if", at the time in question, the Dedicator has the right to grant
      these licences under it without breaching an obligation to, or paying,
      another person (errata E39, E60). It no longer points to Section 13.2.
      (+36 words.)
    * Section 8.6's covenant not to assert the claims Sections 8.1 and 8.4
      reach is now given for every jurisdiction and reaches "any act that
      those Sections describe" (errata E42, E45). It stands on the same
      footing as "the covenants in Section 5", no longer explains the three
      footings, and ends as Section 4.4 and Section 12.2 end (errata E54,
      E71). (-69 words.)
    * Section 1.7, Section 6.4, and Section 8.4 now name "other data"
      resulting from processing after "model, parameter set, weight,
      embedding, output". (+6 words.)
    * Section 9.1 now gives code produced by an automated system as an example
      of Machine-Generated Material. (+7 words.)
    * Section 9.2 now says that where a right this Dedication reaches
      subsists, "this Dedication applies to it", where it listed Sections 3 to
      8 and Section 12 (errata E72). (-3 words.)
    * Section 9.4 and Section 14.2 now say "Section 9" and "Section 14",
      where each referred to itself (errata E57). (0 words.)
    * Section 10.3 now opens "Under this Dedication" (errata E74). (+3 words.)
    * The heading of Section 11 now ends "AND PRIVACY" (errata E59). (+1 word.)
    * Section 11.1 now says "Nothing in Section 11 reduces what any other
      Section of this Dedication gives", where it said "Nothing in this Section
      reduces a permission granted by Sections 3 to 10" (errata E52). (0
      words.)
    * Section 11.4 now opens "This Dedication, in respect of the Work,
      reaches", speaks of "the patent claims Section 8 reaches" (as
      Section 8.6 now also does), and says that a permission the law of data
      protection or privacy requires is not the Dedicator's to give (errata
      E43, E44). Like Section 2.7 and Section 8.5, it no longer points to
      Section 13.2 or states why its limit matters, and it ends with the
      sentence that ends Section 11.3. (-33 words.)
    * Section 12.2 now reaches anyone who receives the Work from You "directly
      or indirectly", the words Sections 4.6 and 5.3 use (errata E62). (+3
      words.)
    * Section 12.2 now also gives, in a sentence of its own, the Dedicator's
      consent to any act by You or by any such person that would otherwise
      infringe those rights; Section 12.3, Section 12.4, and Section 12.5 name
      the consent beside the covenant, and Section 2.4 names consent among the
      grants, permissions, and covenants that stand independently. (+29
      words.)
    * Section 12.5 now says in one sentence that the interest Moral Rights
      protect against false attribution "survives" the covenant in Section 12.2
      (errata E53). (-11 words.)
    * Section 14.1 now excepts any liability for wilful misconduct, gross
      negligence, death, or personal injury that applicable law does not
      permit to be excluded, and says that nothing in Section 14 limits it.
      (+29 words.)
    * Section 14.2 now says that Section 14 does not limit the Dedicator's
      liability for asserting a claim, or exercising a right, in breach of a
      covenant in Section 5, 8.6, or 12.2. (+27 words.)
    * Section 2.6 now says that "This Dedication requires no separate
      instrument, assignment, or agreement" to place a Contribution under it,
      where it said that none "is needed". (0 words.)
    * Section 8.1 and Section 8.4 now name any offer, where they named an
      offer to sell, and also name lending, leasing, exporting, keeping, and
      possessing among the acts they license. (+6 words.)
    * Section 8.2 now says that the licences are not terminable by the
      Dedicator "under this Dedication" on any ground (errata E86). (+3
      words.)
    * Section 8.6 now says that its covenant "is given for every
      jurisdiction", where it said that it "operates" in every jurisdiction.
      (+1 word.)
    * Section 2.6, Section 5.3, Section 6.2, Section 6.3, Section 8.4,
      Section 11.3, Section 11.4, and Section 12.2 now name themselves by
      number. (0 words.)
    * Section 2.5 now says that the Dedicator "covenants not to assert" that
      this Dedication is revocable or ineffective, and Section 14.2 names
      Section 2.5 among the covenants whose breach Section 14 does not limit.
      (+2 words.)
    * Section 5.1 and Section 5.2(d) now speak of a use of the Work that this
      Dedication "permits or purports to permit". (+8 words.)
    * Section 15.3 no longer says that this Dedication is intended to operate
      under the law of each jurisdiction in which the Work is used. (-21
      words.)
    * Section 6.4 and Section 8.4 now say "in each case" before "resulting
      from", and Section 1.7 now says "other data that results from that
      processing". (+7 words.)
    * Section 4.5 now speaks of terms "incompatible with this Dedication",
      where it said "with these". (+1 word.)
    * Section 5.2 now says that Section 5 reaches the terms the Dedicator
      applies to its own material "to the extent they restrict what may be
      done with that copy" of the Work. (+18 words.)
    * Section 8.3 now says that nothing, "as to those acts", reserves,
      preserves, or leaves unaffected any patent claim Section 8.1 and Section
      8.4 reach. (+4 words.)
    * Section 11.2 now says that "Section 11.1 does not restrict nominative
      reference", and no longer says "You may". (0 words.)
    * Section 12.2 no longer replaces Section 12.1 where the law makes
      moral rights personal to the author or incapable of waiver. Its
      covenant and consent are now "given for every jurisdiction, whether or
      not Section 12.1 has effect there", and You are not required to
      determine whether Section 12.1 has effect. (-15 words.)
    * Section 12.3 now says the covenant and the consent are given in
      respect of "the Work, which the Dedicator has chosen to place in the
      commons", where it said "a work". (0 words.)
    * Section 14.1 now says "IN EACH CASE WHERE APPLICABLE LAW DOES NOT PERMIT
      IT TO BE EXCLUDED". (+4 words.)
    * Section 1.9 no longer sets "with a notice of this Dedication" between
      commas. (0 words.)
    * Section 2.5 now places "for want of consideration or formality" before
      "revocable or ineffective". (0 words.)
    * Section 5.2(b) now excepts "trademark rights or rights in a name
      (Section 11.1)", as Section 5.1 does. (+12 words.)
    * Section 6.5 now says that Section 6 "operates on its own footing
      (Section 2.4)" as well as stating what Section 3, Section 4, Section 5,
      and Section 8 would permit. (+9 words.)
    * Section 10.2 now speaks of licensing under "this Dedication or any
      other terms", where it said "these". (+1 word.)
    * Section 2.2 now says that "This Dedication is made irrevocably" and that
      "Nothing in this Dedication permits the Dedicator to withdraw it or to
      impose any further term". (+4 words.)
    * Section 16.2 now says that an identifier is sufficient "notice of this
      Dedication", the words Section 1.9 uses, where it said "of these
      terms". (0 words.)
    * Section 2.6 now says that the undertaking to identify excluded material
      "is subject to Section 14". (+5 words.)
    * Section 3.2 now gives the full term and the rights to remuneration to
      "any waiver under Section 3.3" as well as to the surrender in
      Section 3.1. (+6 words.)
    * Section 5.1 and Section 5.2(b) now except "a payment You agreed to
      make for providing a copy or for a service that is not permission to
      use the Work". (+40 words.)
    * Section 5.3 now names the covenant in Section 2.5 among those that bind
      successors and may be relied upon. (+1 word.)
    * Section 6.3 now says the disclaimer of reliance "is given in every
      case", where it said that it "operates", as Section 8.6 says its
      covenant is given. (+1 word.)
    * Section 10.4 now gives as its reason that this Dedication "attaches no
      condition to any permission (Section 10.1)", where it gave as its
      reason that there was nothing You could breach. (+2 words.)
    * Section 12.2 now covenants also "not to authorise or assist any other
      person to exercise" Moral Rights, as Section 8.6 does. (+11 words.)
    * Section 4.5 now says that it "does not permit a person to impose what
      Section 5 forbids on material as to which that person is a
      Dedicator". (+23 words.)
    * Section 10.1 now imposes nothing upon You in respect of the Work,
      "other than in Your capacity as a Dedicator". (+8 words.)
    * Section 16.2 now speaks of "material to which a Dedicator has applied
      this Dedication", where it said "a work". (-1 word.)
    * Section 11.3 now says that "Retaining a notice that accompanied or was
      contained in the Work is not such a representation". (+16 words.)
    * Section 12.5 now names the waiver in Section 12.1 as well as the
      covenant and the consent in Section 12.2. (+7 words.)
    * Section 5.2 now also authorises circumvention of a measure on a copy
      and waives the power to forbid it. (+78 words.)
    * Section 5.2's authorisation of circumvention now applies "To the
      fullest extent the law permits". (-1 word.)
    * Section 14.2 now opens "Subject to Section 14.1, where applicable law
      does not permit a liability to be excluded". (+2 words.)
    * Section 16.3 now says that this Dedication may be applied "to any
      material", where it said "to any work". (0 words.)
    * Section 4.4 now says that the licence "is granted whether or not
      Section 3 is effective", the words its next sentence uses. (+1 word.)
    * Section 15.2 now says that a provision that cannot be reformed "is to
      be treated as severed", and the remainder "as continuing in full
      force". (+5 words.)
    * Section 12.6 now says that the invalidity "is to be treated as
      affecting no other Section". (+5 words.)
    * Section 6.4 now opens "To the fullest extent the law permits". (+7
      words.)
    * Section 2.6 now states in a sentence of its own that "Section 6.5
      states why it matters". (-1 word.)
    * Section 15.1 now joins its two sentences, and "in respect of the Work"
      now also governs the rule that the reading granting more to You
      prevails, and says on whom the least obligation falls, "upon You"
      (errata E63, E75). (+5 words.)
    * Section 15.5 now says that "person" includes an entity (errata E58). (+4
      words.)
    * Section 16.1 now names the forms in Section 16.2 alongside the long
      notice. (+7 words.)
    * Section 16.2 now speaks of "material to which a Dedicator has applied
      this Dedication", says what material a notice a Dedicator places applies
      this Dedication to, lets a narrower notice of the Dedicator prevail, and
      matches its identifiers without regard to case (errata E69). It also
      says that "a notice that is only a Reservation assigns nothing to other
      terms". (+104 words.)
    * Section 16.3 now reaches any work in which the person applying this
      Dedication holds "or may hold" rights "of their own". (+6 words.)
    * Section 16.4 now restricts any name containing "Autonomous Commons
      Dedication" and any identifier containing "ACD-" followed by a digit,
      "regardless of case", and lets the Steward use them for a modified text
      only as a new version (errata E76, E77). It says that its provisos are
      conditions upon distributing the text and not upon any use of the Work
      (Section 10.5), and applies itself to a new version distributed under
      that exception (errata E80). (+58 words.)
    * Section 16.5 now names, after an assignment, "the person or entity to
      whom that role was last assigned". (+4 words.)
    * Section 16.6 now says that Sections 16.4 and 16.5 bind "no one in respect
      of the Work", where it said they bind no recipient of the Work, with the
      limit "in respect of the Work" that Sections 10.1, 10.4, and 11.4 use
      (errata E48). (+2 words.)
    * ACD-1.3: 5443 words, 80 clauses. This draft: 5876 words, 80 clauses.
