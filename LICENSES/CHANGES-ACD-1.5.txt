ACD-1.5 -- CHANGES FROM ACD-1.4 (DRAFT). Not part of the Dedication.

  The reasons for each change are in LICENSES/ACD-1.5-CHANGELIST.md (in
  Japanese).

  Changes in this draft, relative to ACD-1.4:

    * Restatement of the whole text: what ACD-1.4 says in several places is
      said once; the preamble and the sentences that explain other sentences
      are left out, and the clauses are renumbered
      (LICENSES/CLAUSE-MAP-1.4-1.5.tsv). It covers Sections 1.1 to 1.12, 2.1
      to 2.7, 3.1 to 3.2, 4.1 to 4.4, 5.1 to 5.6, 6.1 to 6.5, 7.1 to 7.4, 8.1
      to 8.2, 9.1 to 9.3, and 10.1 to 10.7. (-2420 words.)
    * ACD-1.4: 5879 words, 80 clauses. This draft: 3459 words, 52 clauses.

  What differs in effect, so that a reader need not compare the two texts:

    1. A Dedicator is each person who applies the Dedication, whether or not a
       right subsists; as to each Dedicator, the Work is the material to which
       that Dedicator applied it, and the text of the Dedication is not part of
       it (Sections 1.2 and 1.3; errata E93).
    2. Moral Rights include those of a performer (Section 1.5; errata E92).
    3. A person bound as a successor or transferee is not a Dedicator as to
       what that person holds otherwise than from the Dedicator (Section 1.3),
       and utility models are named (Section 1.7).
    4. A Result may result from the Work indirectly, and a copy of the Work is
       not itself a Result (Section 1.8).
    5. The patent licence reaches subject matter in the Work, which is the
       material to which the Dedicator applied the Dedication, including, where
       the Work is a Contribution, by its combination with the material it was
       submitted for inclusion in, and an infringement on any theory (Section
       2.3(a); errata E89).
    6. A feature of a modified version that the Work itself did not teach or
       embody is governed by Section 2.3(a) alone; in ACD-1.4 Section 8.1 alone
       governed every feature added to the Work (Section 2.3(b)).
    7. Everyone who receives the Work, anything derived from it, or a Result
       holds the licences directly from the Dedicator (Section 2.4).
    8. The covenants as to Moral Rights and as to other claims reach what the
       Dedicator holds (Section 2.5(c)), and a claim that the Dedicator holds or
       controls, or that another holds through the Dedicator (Section 2.5(d)).
    9. The covenant as to other claims is about a claim that treats a use as
       wrongful by reason of its being a use of the Work, or, as to the
       Dedicator's own likeness, voice, name, or performance contained in the
       Work, under a right of privacy, publicity, or personality; a claim under
       the law of data protection, which the general words of ACD-1.4's Section
       5.1 covered without naming it, is outside it (Section 2.5(d);
       ACD-1.1-SELF-AUDIT.md, sections 3zf and 3zh).
   10. Nothing is imposed in respect of a Result, as well as of the Work; and a
       person bound as a transferee is named beside the Dedicator where the
       absence of conditions, and the freedom to choose terms, do not reach
       (Sections 4.1 and 4.3; ACD-1.1-SELF-AUDIT.md, section 3ze).
   11. The covenant on technological measures authorises circumvention for a
       copy that the Dedicator has made available (Section 5.2).
   12. The covenant not to assert a narrower reading of a permission (ACD-1.4's
       Section 5.2(c)) is left out; Section 9.1 governs the reading.
   13. The exceptions from the covenants are stated covenant by covenant
       (Sections 5.4 to 5.6). A term about a Contribution, other than one
       restricting use of the Work, asked of the person submitting it, is
       outside the covenant on collateral obligations, and so are terms for the
       use of a site or service generally, other than a registration or
       acceptance required in order to obtain a copy (Section 5.4(d) and (e)).
   14. The omission of attribution alone is not a false statement (Section
       6.3).
   15. The statement in ACD-1.4's Section 11.4 that the Dedication reaches
       nothing else is gone (ACD-1.1-SELF-AUDIT.md, section 3zd).
   16. Neither passing on or recording another person's copy or notice with the
       material it identifies, nor mentioning the Dedication otherwise than to
       apply it, applies it to anything (Section 10.2; errata E90).
   17. Each person who made and submits a Contribution, or on whose authority it
       is submitted, applies the Dedication to it, unless that person states
       otherwise (Section 10.4).
   18. ACD-1.4's preamble, its Section 8.3, which says that no patent claim is
       reserved, the sentences of purpose in its Sections 6.5 and 12.3, the
       sentence of its Section 9.2 that the Dedication adds nothing where no
       right subsists, and the sentence of its Section 16.3 that it is not
       specific to any project, person, or field, are left out.
   19. ACD-1.4's Section 6.4 says that no enforceable claim arises in respect
       of a model or an output. Here that is a covenant (Section 2.5(a)) and an
       absence of conditions (Section 4.1).
   20. A Contribution is any material, not only a work of authorship, and not
       material submitted to a repository or forum that puts such submissions
       under other terms instead (Section 1.11; ACD-1.1-SELF-AUDIT.md, section
       3zg).
   21. The warranties and the kinds of loss are named in shorter lists
       (Sections 8.1 and 8.2).
   22. The suggested notice says that the work is provided as is, and points to
       Section 2.5(d) (Section 10.1).
   23. A person to whom a Covered Right or a Patent Claim is later exclusively
       licensed is named beside a transferee (Section 7.2(b)).
   24. The Dedication may be applied by placing a copy of its text with the
       material as its licence (Section 10.1).
